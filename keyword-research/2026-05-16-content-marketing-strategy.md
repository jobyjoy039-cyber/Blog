## SERP Analysis

### "content marketing strategy small business 2026"
**Top results:** GreenCupDigital (niche agency), Kate&Co, Verve Creative, WordStream
**DA spread:** Mix of niche agencies and established tools (WordStream) → **Medium**
**SERP features confirmed:** PAA box present, no featured snippet locked → opportunity
**Content gap:** All top results are generic frameworks. None offers an actual
week-by-week implementation plan for a solo blogger or tiny team.
**Recommended angle:** "90-Day Content Marketing Plan for Solo Bloggers: Week-by-Week"

### "content marketing strategy template beginner"
**Top results:** CoSchedule, HubSpot, Semrush, Backlinko, Canva — very high DA
**DA spread:** Almost entirely DA 80+ → **Hard** for broad match
**SERP features confirmed:** Featured snippet owned by Semrush
**Content gap:** All templates are generic. None is tailored to bloggers specifically
(vs enterprise or social media teams). Blogger-specific template is the gap.
**Recommended angle:** "Content Marketing Template for Bloggers (Not Agencies)"

### "content marketing trends 2026"
**Top results:** WordStream, Impact Plus, Content Whale — mix
**DA spread:** Mix → **Medium-Hard**
**SERP features confirmed:** PAA box with 3 questions on authenticity and AI
**Content gap:** Most trend articles are roundups without strategic depth. Gap:
a post that tells you *what to do differently* based on each trend, not just
what the trend is.
**Recommended angle:** "8 Content Marketing Trends in 2026 (and What to Do About Each)"
