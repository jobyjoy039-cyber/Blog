## SERP Analysis

### "blog writing tips for SEO 2026"
**Top results:** Yoast, Bynder, Semrush, Content Whale, Elementor
**DA spread:** Mix of DA 80+ (Yoast, Semrush, Elementor) + niche agencies → **Hard**
**SERP features confirmed:** Featured snippet, PAA box = high competition signal
**Content gap:** Every top result is a generic numbered list. None offers a
structured, replicable **workflow** or a tool-specific automation angle.
**Recommended angle:** "The Blog Writing Workflow: From Brief to Published in
One Session" — process-first, not tips-first.

### "how to write SEO blog post that ranks"
**Top results:** Semrush (blog post guide), TrySight.ai, River Editor, InfluenceFlow
**DA spread:** Mix of high DA (Semrush) and new niche sites → **Medium-Hard**
**SERP features confirmed:** PAA box present with 4+ questions → opportunity
**Content gap:** Question-map methodology is mentioned but never demonstrated
end-to-end.
**Recommended angle:** "Question Map Method: How to Write SEO Blog Posts That
Match Intent Exactly" — methodology-first, differentiated from list posts.

### "AI citation optimization blog 2026"
**Top results:** Frase.io, Jasper, Enrich Labs, Opollo (emerging niche sites)
**DA spread:** Mostly niche/medium sites → **Medium** difficulty
**SERP features confirmed:** No featured snippet locked up yet → open opportunity
**Content gap:** All results focus on *paid tools* (Semrush AI, MarketMuse).
Nobody has written a practical guide for doing GEO/AEO *without* a paid tool.
**Recommended angle:** "GEO Without Semrush: Free Techniques to Get Your Blog
Cited by AI Search" — underserved audience angle.
