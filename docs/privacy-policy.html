PixelBoost Privacy Policy

App: PixelBoost AI Upscaler (com.jobyjoy.pixelboost). Effective date: 24 September 2026.

Summary

PixelBoost processes your images entirely on your device. It doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data, and it has no internet access.

Images you choose

When you pick or share an image with PixelBoost, the app reads it only to upscale it on your phone. The image and the result stay on your device. Results are written to your gallery (Pictures/PixelBoost) only when you tap Save, and are sent to another app only when you tap Share and choose that app.

Data collection

PixelBoost doesn't collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics or crash reports. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The app doesn't request the Internet permission, so it can't send data anywhere.

Permissions

On Android 9 and older only, the app asks for storage access so it can save results to your gallery. No other permissions are used.

Children

PixelBoost isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
